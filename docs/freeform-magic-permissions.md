# Freeform magic: domain permissions

Research result, 26 October 2026. Companion to the
[extension plan](freeform-magic-plan.md). These are source-grounded permission
summaries in neutral language. The separate [coverage ledger](magic/coverage.json) maps them to implemented operations.

Source: `freeform-1994`, third-generation adaptation, 1994, printing unverified.
Printed pp. 97–127 contain the introduction and all 45 rank descriptions;
additional examples continue through p. 128. Printed page numbers are used below;
PDF viewer pages are one higher. The original PDF's SHA-256 matches the reference
catalogue: `9052542535e6ee826fa7f12abf1b46ab13a332b3d38788524ba8347c0863b6d0`.
The relevant rank descriptions were read using OCR and checked against page
images. The private reference library now has a separate editorial synthesis of
the same 45 rank descriptions; this document keeps the extension's neutral terms.

There is **no single printed permission matrix**. These tables organize the
book's level descriptions and qualifications. Page 97 treats them as guidance:
examples are not exhaustive, methods matter, and the referee assigns requirements
to effects not explicitly covered. For a computer game, unresolved judgments
become authored permission rules, not guesses from a verb or domain name.

Each rank adds capabilities; earlier ones remain available. Rank alone does not
bypass sensing, casting, scale, duration, defenses, resources or consequences.
Named example effects, organizations, characters and cosmology are omitted.

## Space

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sense nearby spatial arrangements, distances, volumes and shapes without ordinary senses. | Deliberately hidden things still require the ordinary discovery procedure. | 98 |
| 2 | Extend ordinary senses to another location. | Limited tactile information; remote observation creates a detectable spatial disturbance. | 98 |
| 3 | Relocate yourself, perceive several locations simultaneously, or search across space for a known target. | Destination hazards still apply. Simultaneous perceptions can overload the caster; searching requires identifying information. | 98–99 |
| 4 | Relocate other people or objects, join places with a passage, or place your body in several locations. | Ordinary passages require willing passage or being carried through; they do not automatically mix environments. A distributed body is affected by every occupied location. | 99–100 |
| 5 | Alter distances and dimensions, overlap whole locations, or open passages that mix environments. | Size changes do not themselves change mass or momentum; additional domains are needed. | 100 |

Relocation is not the same operation as moving continuously through intervening
surroundings. Enlargement is not a free increase in mass.

## Chance and decay

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Detect weak points, instability and disorder in physical things or organized activity. | Detection does not itself damage or dismantle the target. | 102–103 |
| 2 | Influence small random events and how much disorder an actual event introduces. | Cannot invent a randomizing event from nothing or remove randomness absolutely. | 103 |
| 3 | Accelerate or restrain nonliving deterioration and energy dispersal. | Alters a process or rate; does not grant absolute annihilation or perfect elimination of disorder. | 103 |
| 4 | Affect aging, deterioration, growth and random processes in living organisms. | Precise selection of bodily functions additionally needs life knowledge; this passage does not assign that additional rank. | 104 |
| 5 | Erode a particular person's ideas, reasoning or feelings. | Not permission to erase a philosophy throughout a society. Required success depends on complexity and strength of belief. | 104 |

Rank 3 is not an unrestricted instant-disintegration command. Scale and time
remain separate from permission.

## Energy

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Perceive and measure physical energies beyond ordinary sensory ranges. | The radiation or other energy being sensed must exist. | 105–106 |
| 2 | Redirect existing electromagnetic radiation, electricity and magnetism. | Cannot add to the available energy. | 106–107 |
| 3 | Create those forms of energy. | Requires power 2. | 107 |
| 4 | Control existing gravity, motion, heat, sound and the source's category of chemical/nuclear forces. | Cannot create extra energy. Redirecting motion requires existing motion and cannot increase speed. The text excludes releasing an atom's explosive energy at this rank. | 107 |
| 5 | Generate the forms of energy covered at rank 4. | Requires power 2; allows initiating motion, not merely redirecting it. Does not imply unlimited scale. | 107 |

These are this edition's game categories, not modern scientific classifications.
Do not substitute a later edition's progression. Gravity and kinetic control
are rank 4 here, not rank 2.

## Life

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Detect organisms and examine their health and biological characteristics. | Diagnosis does not itself heal. | 109 |
| 2 | Reshape, injure or heal simple organisms. | Includes plants, microorganisms and generally invertebrates. Sensory access is still required. | 109–110 |
| 3 | Alter and heal your body; create familiar simple organisms. | Creation requires power 2. Created organisms have instinctive responses, not newly created conscious minds. | 110, 112 |
| 4 | Alter other complex organisms, including people. | Includes bodily repair, harm and modification; complete transformation remains rank 5. Prolonged alteration may cause continuing injury. | 110 |
| 5 | Create complex organisms and wholly new viable forms; transform yourself or another creature into another living form. | Creation requires power 2 and supplies neither a conscious mind nor a spirit. Transformation preserves intelligence and can cause continuing injury. | 111–112 |

Page 110 also limits healing attempts against the same injury state, including
attempts by successive healers. Cooperative healing is possible; new injury or
an authorized change of condition can reopen treatment. Some injuries cannot be
magically healed, including backlash injury and injury from unstable bodily
alteration. The self-enhancement example cannot become permanent through its
duration roll. A wholly invented organism requires rank 5 even if otherwise
biologically simple, and must be viable (p. 112).

The [direct source audit](freeform-magic-source-audit.md) adds the two-health-points
per success healing guideline and the overt healing surcharge for severe wounds
(p. 137). The narrower natural-healing-only exceptions above still apply. It also
records the specific life-damage formula, which differs from the general chart.

## Matter

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Identify material composition/properties and perceive structures concealed by other material. | Perceives nonliving matter, not every kind of hidden entity. | 112–113 |
| 2 | Create an object or quantity of one material. | Requires power 2; material must be stable under surrounding conditions unless another effect supplies suitable conditions. | 113 |
| 3 | Reshape matter, alter density or phase thresholds, break structures apart, or make selected materials cease interacting. | Single-material limits apply to the described operations; changing a phase threshold does not change actual temperature. | 113–114 |
| 4 | Change a substance into another while retaining shape; create objects made from several materials. | Creation still requires power 2. The transmutation example affects one material type at a time. | 114 |
| 5 | Make novel substances with extraordinary properties and extremely small complex structures. | No unlimited density or absolute immunity to thermal/electromagnetic interaction. Creation retains the general power requirement. | 114–115 |

The source classifies corpses as matter (p. 112); living organisms use life.
Any undead category in a particular game needs an explicit author policy.

The p. 114 special mass table belongs to one matter-2 plus power-2 creation
formula; matter 4 later expands its material composition. Do not attach that
formula's scale to every matter-4 effect. The source's general mass rule
expressly yields to a specific operation's scale (p. 89).

## Mind

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Enhance your mental functions, memory, calculation or capacity for simultaneous tasks. | Selected benefits only; extra mental tasks do not permit simultaneous casting. Permanent benefits require character-point payment. | 115–116 |
| 2 | Sense nearby minds and emotions; send subconscious suggestions, images or words. | Not full conscious conversation or unrestricted control. | 116 |
| 3 | Communicate consciously, inspect thoughts/memories, project sensory illusions or make psychic attacks. | Resistance and effect-specific limits apply; established links can persist when a subject moves away. | 117 |
| 4 | Control another mind/body, alter mental processes, and suppress or fabricate memories. | The memory example blocks access to existing memories rather than deleting them; it is reversible. | 117–118 |
| 5 | Separate consciousness from the body, travel mentally and attach it to another suitable body. | Does not supply physical senses, a new body or travel through other worlds. | 118 |

The rank-1 enhancement example has an exception to general scaling (pp. 115–116):
allocate success separately to the benefit and its duration, with at least one
point for duration. The benefit calculation uses source character-point prices,
which need host conversion. The example excludes willpower increases, empathy
and psychic powers from its generic selection.

Retry conditions also vary by operation. The thought-probing example cannot be
repeated on a subject until they sleep; a failed possession attempt has the same
reopening condition (p. 117). Do not generalize either rule to every mental effect.
Thought probing lasts minutes equal to success margin; memory alteration uses
the duration chart for the amount of memory affected. Possession always attempts
resistance. Projection has its own perception, attributes and Will-based injury
rules. The [parallel source audit](freeform-magic-source-audit.md#parallel-source-audit-26-october-2026)
records these details; rank permission alone is insufficient to implement them.

## Power

**Extension override, adopted 26 October 2026 (M20-02):** absorbing portable
stored reserve requires power rank 3. This replaces the earlier source's rank-1
consumption permission (p. 80), using the anniversary prerequisite (pp. 332,
520-521). It does not raise the separate receiving/storage or ordinary source
meditation requirements. The table below continues to describe the earlier
published source; the override governs the planned extension.

**Extension addition, adopted 26 October 2026 (M20-05):** a successful power-5
working may remove the caster's own accumulated strain by spending one reserve
per temporary strain point removed (anniversary p. 549). The adopted lasting
strain exception (M20-08b) requires a separately authored resolution; this working
cannot remove it. This expressly extends the
earlier source's permission, which addresses external manifestations. The
rank-5 limitation in the source table below is therefore overridden for vhyl.

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sense magical supply and receive/store reserve beyond the innate amount. | Receiving is not extraction or transfer; the example allows one receiving roll per source. | 119 |
| 2 | Supply the magical basis for creating life, matter or energy; disturb a living target's sustaining flow. | Does not fill personal reserve from an unlimited background supply. The disturbance example requires affecting the whole target's mass. | 120 |
| 3 | Move existing transferable reserve between receptacles; qualify to create enchanted items. | Cannot take protected innate reserve, conjure surplus reserve, or bypass item-creation skill requirements. | 120 |
| 4 | Remove the underlying support of nonliving matter or energy, making it cease existing. | Released supply is not collected as usable reserve. Selective property removal also needs relevant matter/energy knowledge; exact additional ranks are not specified here. | 120–121 |
| 5 | Alter living beings' sustaining flow, replenish innate reserve and affect certain external manifestations of strain. | Replenishment is capped by channel rating; no general permission to erase personal accumulated strain. | 121 |

Storage, transfer and replenishment are different permissions. Access to a supply
does not give a rank-1 recipient the ability to extract it. Periodic production
and full item construction are recovered in the companion source audit. Taking
another caster's excess reserve is explicitly permitted, with applicable direct
resistance. The power-3 plus space-4 remote-delivery formula moves one reserve
per success, rather than the general five-per-success transfer (pp. 120-121).

## Spirits

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Perceive a neighboring nonphysical world, spirits and boundary strength; detect observation across the boundary. | Looking across can replace physical awareness; difficulty depends on the local barrier. | 122 |
| 2 | Call and communicate with spirits; perceive more distant nonphysical regions. | Does not compel arrival, friendliness or obedience. | 122 |
| 3 | Cross personally into the neighboring nonphysical world and return. | Requires a barrier contest. Critical failure can trap the traveler. Does not grant unrestricted carriage of belongings. | 122–123 |
| 4 | Build spirit-containing fields/vessels, form enclosed other-world regions, and strengthen or breach boundaries. | Region creation also needs matter, power and supply; exact ranks are not assigned here. Vessel construction refers to another source. A breach does not control who uses it. | 123 |
| 5 | Travel beyond the neighboring world, survive temporarily in hostile nonphysical regions and place spiritual identifying marks. | Protection expires; destinations and the significance of marks are authored world content. | 124 |

These permissions do not require the source's named worlds or geography. The
author still decides whether spirits, boundaries and other worlds exist.
The source also supplies sight thresholds, boundary changes of two per success,
trapping/rescue, and creator-controlled breach closure (pp. 122-124). Projection
to distant nonphysical worlds requires spirits 5 plus mind 5. Common spirit
powers and manifestation statistics are on pp. 167 and 169. Spirit-vessel
construction is **deferred for later review** (user decision, 26 October 2026):
the referenced book is not owned. Retain this rank permission but mark its
construction procedure unsupported; it does not block the core extension. These
other procedures are recovered in the companion audit.

## Time

| Rank | Added permission | Qualification | Printed pages |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sense temporal disturbances and measure time precisely. | Requires a separately purchased accurate-timekeeping trait; magical timing goes beyond that trait. | 125 |
| 2 | Observe the past or possible futures. | Past observation concerns a location; future information is uncertain. Time range and viewing duration are separate. | 125–126 |
| 3 | Speed or slow local time. | Examples have a skill penalty, margin-based rate changes and an area no larger than a small room. | 126 |
| 4 | Suspend local events and arrange for them or other effects to resume later. | Bounded by duration and area; outside interference can break a suspension. | 126 |
| 5 | Move forward through time or act temporarily outside ordinary time. | Does not permit physical travel into the past or make frozen powered machinery operate. | 126–127 |

Host-design implications: time travel is not undo/save restoration. Forward
travel, suspension and acceleration need explicit scheduler semantics.
Precognition needs authored possibilities, not access to future random draws.

catalogue of everything possible.

| Create one-material objects | matter 2 + power 2 | 113–114 |
| Create multi-material objects | matter 4 + power 2; general creation requirement plus rank-4 example | 113–114 |
| Change mass with spatial size | Add life or matter; exact additional rank not stated here | 100 |

General descriptions and particular examples do not always reduce to one uniform
algorithm. Preserve explicit examples as exceptions when they differ from an
apparent general minimum, and flag the interaction for author review. Do not
silently correct the source using remembered rules from another edition.

## Remaining work

The basic nine-domain, five-rank permission gap is resolved. The research now
includes principal permissions, explicit combinations, a timekeeping purchase
dependency and important creation, healing, resource and duration restrictions.

Executable coverage still requires an operation map, authored rulings where the
book delegates judgment, host trait/injury conversion and casting/defense
decisions. General scale/damage tables and item construction have now been
recovered in the [source audit](freeform-magic-source-audit.md). Named examples have
not been turned into a complete formula catalogue. References to other books do
not establish those books' mechanics.

Represent each implemented operation with target type, existing-versus-created
material/energy, self-versus-other, required ranks, special margin allocation,
retry reopening condition and supported delivery handler. An unauthored
combination should return `unsupported-effect`, distinct from
`insufficient-rank`. This makes the computer game's adjudication boundary clear.
